Port Jam – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Port Jam ("the game") is a mobile puzzle game made by Efe Akın ("I", "me"). This policy explains what information the game collects, why, and the choices you have. The game does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email address, phone number or location.

1. Information stored on your device

Your game progress (current level, coins, lives, boosters, unlocked skins and settings) is saved only on your device. It is not sent to me. Deleting the game deletes this data.

2. Analytics (GameAnalytics)

To understand how the game is played and to improve it (for example, to find levels that are too hard), the game uses GameAnalytics, an analytics service. GameAnalytics collects:

  • a random identifier for your installation of the game (on iOS, the identifier for vendor; it is not the advertising identifier),
  • device and app information such as device model, operating system version, app version and language,
  • an approximate country, derived from your IP address,
  • gameplay events such as levels started, completed and failed, boosters used, in-game coins earned and spent, and session start and length,
  • technical error reports, if the game runs into an error.

This data is not linked to your identity and is not used to track you across other companies' apps or websites. It is not sold. You can read how GameAnalytics handles data in the GameAnalytics Privacy Policy.

3. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game schedules reminders on your device (for example, when your lives are full or a daily reward is ready). These notifications are created on the device itself; no notification data is sent to me.

4. Advertising and purchases

The current version of the game does not show ads and does not offer in-app purchases. If a future version adds ads or purchases, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the stores' privacy labels will be updated too.

5. Children

The game is suitable for all ages but is not directed specifically at children under 13. I do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The analytics described above do not identify a person.

6. Your choices

  • You can turn notifications off at any time in your device settings.
  • You can delete all locally saved data by deleting the game.
  • To ask for analytics data linked to your installation to be deleted, contact me at the address below.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
